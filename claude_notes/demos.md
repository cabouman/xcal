# Demos

## Simulation demos

| Demo | What it shows | Runtime |
|---|---|---|
| demo/demo_simulated_multi_voltage.py | Reflection tube at 3 voltages, 4 rods.  Recovers the takeoff angle, Al filter, and CsI scintillator from noisy simulated scans. | ~3 min |
| demo/demo_simulated_multi_filtration.py | Synchrotron with 2 filtrations, 2 rods.  Recovers the Si and Al filter thicknesses and picks LuAG from 7 scintillators. | ~1 min |
